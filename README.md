# Sugarplum Plushies 🧸

*A Sugarplum Studios mod for Minecraft Java 1.20.1 (Forge 47.x)*

Fifteen pastel plushies to sew, collect, display, name and cuddle. There are 60 variants in all
(three colourways plus a rare **Sparkle Edition** per plushie), a Plushie Workshop to sew them in,
furniture to show them off, and a collection book with milestone rewards.

---

## Plushies

Every plushie is a real 3D model built from soft, bevelled cuboids with embroidered pixel-art faces.
On cuddle it plays its own animation, makes its own sound and gives off its own particles. While idle
it breathes, bobs, sways or hovers, and wings, tails, gills and halos animate on their own.

## Controls

| Action | What it does |
|---|---|
| Place a plushie | It turns to face you (16 directions) |
| Right-click (empty hand) | Cuddle: animation, sound, particles and a personality message |
| Sneak + right-click (empty hand) | Pick the plushie back up, keeping its variant and name |
| Right-click with a **Brush** | Turn 22.5° clockwise (sneak: anticlockwise) |
| Right-click with a renamed **Name Tag** | Name the plushie; the name floats above it (anvil-renamed plushies keep their names too) |
| Middle-click | Pick-block gives the exact variant |
| Break | Drops the plushie with its variant and name |

## Furniture

| Block | Holds | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Plushie Shelf | 2 plushies | Wall shelf with lace trim. Place it against a wall. |
| Plushie Display Stand | 1 plushie | Lace-topped pedestal that slowly turns like a turntable. Brush toggles spinning. |
| Plushie Bed (Pink / Lavender / Mint / Sky) | 1 plushie | The plushie leans back on the pillow and snores tiny Zzz's. |
| Miniature Display Cabinet | 4 plushies | Glass-fronted cabinet with two shelves and heart wallpaper. |
| Plushie Gift Box (Pink / Lavender / Mint / Sky) | 9 slots | Polka-dot box with a satin bow. Opens like a dispenser. |
| Golden / Rainbow Collector's Trophy | — | Collection rewards. Glows and sparkles. |

How they work: right-click with a plushie to display it, right-click empty-handed to cuddle the
displayed plushie (it animates in place), and sneak + right-click to take it back. Comparators read
how full a display is.

### Plushies (Plushie Workshop)

Open the Workshop, pick a pattern and press **Sew!** (shift-click sews up to 16). Ingredients come
straight from your inventory. Green slots mean you have enough, red means you're short, and a pink
dot marks plushies you haven't collected yet.

Abbreviations: F = Soft Fabric, S = Plush Stuffing, E = Button Eyes, T = Embroidery Thread,
R = Satin Ribbon, ★ = Sugar Star.

**Sparkle Edition upgrade:** any colourway of that plushie + 1 Sugar Star + 2 Embroidery Thread
+ 4 gold nuggets. The Workshop uses ordinary copies first and never consumes the plushie you're
upgrading into. It only takes a named plushie or a Sparkle Edition if nothing else matches.

Rarity scales the cost. Commons need early-game materials, while Rares and the Epic dragon need
gold, glowstone, lapis, amethyst, phantom membranes or a Sugar Star. Wandering traders sell Sugar
Stars (5 emeralds), Soft Fabric and the odd plushie. Every price is fixed and shown up front:
**no randomised loot boxes and no real-money mechanics.**

## Collection system

* Any plushie that lands in your inventory, from any source, is recorded per variant and pops a
  "New plushie collected!" toast. Progress is stored on the player and survives death.
* **Plushie Collection Book**: a grid of all 15 plushies (uncollected ones show as silhouettes),
  four variant dots each, species/variant counters and a progress bar. Click a plushie to see its
  rarity, personality and which variants you own.
* **Rewards tab**: fixed rewards, claimed with a button.

| Milestone | Requirement | Reward |
|---|---|---|
| Cuddle Starter | 3 plushies | 6 Soft Fabric, 6 Plush Stuffing, 2 Button Eyes |
| Shelf Stylist | 6 plushies | 2 Plushie Shelves, Display Stand |
| Sweet Collector | 9 plushies | 2 Sugar Stars, Display Cabinet |
| Plushie Curator | 12 plushies | 4 Sugar Stars, Pink Plushie Bed |
| Sugarplum Master | all 15 plushies | Golden Collector's Trophy, 4 Sugar Stars |
| Sparkle Hunter | 5 Sparkle Editions | 6 Sugar Stars |
| Grand Collector | all 60 variants | Rainbow Collector's Trophy |

There are also advancements: *A New Friend*, *Dear Diary...*, *Sparkle Edition!*,
*Here Be (Cute) Dragons*, *Sugarplum Master* and *Every Last Stitch*.

## Configuration

`config/sugarplumplushies-common.toml`: cuddle messages, cuddle particles, cuddle cooldown,
sneak pick-up, whether name tags are used up, collection rewards, inventory scan interval and
wandering-trader trades.

`config/sugarplumplushies-client.toml`: idle animations on/off, animation speed, ambient particles,
when name plates show (`ALWAYS` / `LOOKING` / `NEVER`), display-stand spin and its speed, and
unlock toasts.

## Building & testing

Requirements: JDK 17 (Gradle downloads one automatically if missing) and an internet connection
the first time.

```bash
./gradlew build              # -> build/libs/sugarplumplushies-forge-1.20.1-1.0.0.jar
./gradlew runClient          # dev client with the mod loaded
./gradlew runClient -Pshowcase  # builds a showcase world, screenshots it into run/screenshots/ and quits
./gradlew runGameTestServer  # headless behaviour tests (6 GameTests)
```

### In-game test checklist

1. Make a creative world and open the **Sugarplum Plushies** creative tab. It has every plushie
   variant, all furniture and all materials.
2. Place a few plushies. Check that they face you, breathe or bob, and that wings, tails, gills,
   halos and the moon animate.
3. Right-click each one: it plays its own animation, sound and particles, with a pink message above
   the hotbar.
4. Use a Brush to turn a plushie. Use a renamed Name Tag on it and look at it to see the name plate.
   Sneak + right-click with an empty hand to pick it up (name and variant are kept).
5. Place a Shelf on a wall, a Stand, a Bed and a Cabinet. Right-click with plushies to fill them,
   cuddle the plushies on display, and sneak + right-click to take them back.
6. In survival, craft a Plushie Workshop and materials, sew a Pink Teddy Bear, then upgrade it with
   a Sugar Star. Watch for the toast and the advancement.
7. Craft the Collection Book: check the grid, variant dots and counters, then claim *Cuddle Starter*
   once you have 3 plushies.
8. At night, check that Moon Cat and Sparkle Editions glow and twinkle.

## Project layout

```
src/main/java/com/sugarplumstudios/plushies/
  plushie/      species data, rarities, cuddle and idle animations
  block/        plushie block, display furniture, gift box, workshop, trophy
  item/         plushie item (variant in BlockStateTag), materials, collection book
  recipe/ menu/ Workshop recipe type and menu
  collection/   per-player collection data, milestones, inventory scanning
  network/      sync / unlock-toast / claim packets
  client/       renderers (animated BER), particles, book & workshop screens, toast
  gametest/     headless behaviour tests
  dev/          showcase harness for -Pshowcase (development only, left out of the jar)
tools/generate_assets.py + tools/assetgen/
  procedural asset pipeline: models, textures, blockstates, recipes, loot, lang
```

All models, textures and data files are generated by `python tools/generate_assets.py`
(Python 3, standard library only). To change a plushie's shape, colours, face or animated
parts, edit `tools/assetgen/plushies.py` and run the script again. It rewrites
`assets/sugarplumplushies` and `data/sugarplumplushies` completely, and keeps the pivots in
`plushie_parts.json` in sync with the geometry.

**Rendering approach:** the project started empty, so it doesn't use GeckoLib. Plushies are
vanilla JSON models drawn by a block-entity renderer, which gives free 16-way rotation, pose-based
cuddle and idle animations, and separately animated part models for wings, tails, gills, halos and
the moon. No extra dependency is needed.
